## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
Rolepaper is a classifieds desk for software, data, security, and electrical engineering jobs that already exist on other boards. We show the facts, date them, and send the candidate to apply with the employer.

## Users
Developers and engineers in Pakistan, the Gulf, and remote-friendly markets who are tired of mega-boards. They drink tea at a kitchen table at 7am and want a dense, honest list, not a consumer app.

## Brand Personality
Newsprint, red pencil, no fluff. We sound like a jobs page in a city paper that learned HTML.

## Anti-references
- LinkedIn blue
- Indeed purple
- “10,000 jobs near you” counters
- Doorway city pages
- Inter / Space Grotesk SaaS careers microsites

## Strategic principles
1. Third-party links only. We are not hiring.
2. Live public APIs, not visitor-side scraping.
3. Unique copy on hubs. Listings can be machine-fetched; hubs cannot be empty.
4. Career pages carry ads later. Listing pages stay readable.
5. Expire stale postings.
