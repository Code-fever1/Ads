# DESIGN.md

## Visual theme
Breakfast classifieds. Warm newsprint, ink, a stamp-red apply mark. Dense tables. Not a card grid of avatars.

## Color
Strategy: full palette, newspaper.

- Newsprint: oklch(0.955 0.018 95)
- Ink: oklch(0.20 0.02 40)
- Rule: oklch(0.78 0.02 90)
- Stamp: oklch(0.52 0.19 28)
- Flag: oklch(0.93 0.04 85)

## Typography
- UI and tables: Atkinson Hyperlegible
- Essays: Libre Baskerville
- Dense listings, generous career-article measure (65–70ch)

## Layout
Masthead + index of desks (software, electrical, remote, career). Jobs as a table. Job detail as a clipping with a red apply stamp.

## Motion
None on layout. Apply button fill only.
