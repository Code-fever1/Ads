# DESIGN.md

## Visual theme
Late-afternoon paper catalog. Ochre banners, soot-black type, numbered entries. Not a dark terminal, not cream SaaS.

## Color
Strategy: Committed ochre on warm paper.

- Paper: oklch(0.965 0.022 88)
- Ink: oklch(0.23 0.035 52)
- Ochre: oklch(0.72 0.155 72)
- Soot: oklch(0.30 0.03 50)
- Rule: oklch(0.82 0.03 80)
- Link rust: oklch(0.48 0.12 38)

## Typography
- Display / UI: Commissioner
- Long copy: Source Serif 4
- Scale: clamp fluid headings, body 1.05rem / 1.6, max 70ch for essays

## Layout
Left-aligned catalog. Index numbers instead of icon grids. Tight clusters, wide breaks between sections. Comparison pages use real tables, not stacked cards.

## Motion
No layout animation. Link underline and ochre fill ease-out-quart only.
