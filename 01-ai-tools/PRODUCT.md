# PRODUCT.md

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
Kiln is a curated directory of AI software. Each listing is a short field note: what the tool is actually for, what it costs in bands, when to skip it, and the date we last checked the vendor page. We send traffic to the vendor. We are not the seller.

## Users
Independent developers, operators, and small-team leads who already tried ChatGPT and now need a specialist. They compare pricing tabs in daylight, often on a laptop in a home office in South Asia or a similar freelance timezone. They distrust undated “top 50 AI tools” dumps.

## Brand Personality
Warm, mechanical, opinionated. Like a mail-order electronics catalog that still uses part numbers. We rank things. We date things. We refuse cracked downloads and miracle claims.

## Anti-references
- Purple-glow “AI startup” landing pages
- Infinite identical icon cards
- Fake “tested by our 40-person lab” without a method
- Inter / Space Grotesk / glassmorphism SaaS kits

## Strategic principles
1. One niche: AI tools and software comparisons only.
2. Last-verified dates are part of the product.
3. Comparison and “best for” pages carry unique copy, not a shuffled list.
4. Affiliate links are labeled. Ads stay off listing density.
5. Dead tools move to sunset, not leftover ranking slots.
